# Reference map: what to take from where (Veronika's full list)

The rule for every project: **the site sells first, beauty comes second.** References are a source of tools, not a goal. Every borrowed device has to answer the client's business question ("more bookings", "more orders"). If a device doesn't help a visitor understand, trust or act, we don't take it, however good it looks.

## Order of work and sources for each layer
Go through every layer and take something from at least one source per layer. Write down what you took in the project brief under "References used": source → what we took → which business goal it serves.

| Layer | Sources | What to take |
|---|---|---|

## Motion budget (strict)
- **One signature move per site**, tied to the main value: the view, the product, the result.
- **Micro-motion**: hover, pressed, loading, success. Calm, the same everywhere.
- **Everything else stays still.** No text appearing on scroll in every block, no parallax "because it's pretty", no cursor tricks unless they help.
- Before adding any animation, answer the question: "what does the visitor understand or do faster because of it?" No answer means no animation.
- Check with `tools/feel-check.js`: smoothness and nothing jumping.
