# Veronika's taste: her own references (23.10). READ before the first screen of any project

Sources: Maison Rouge (floral atelier, Pinterest), GAZU (fashion), Bilexer (Web3), Epoch Savour (perfume, brand board), Evora Design (studio), Behance: Velore Atelier, Lune Noire, Adwise, Nina Elster "Unhurried"; HorizonX: ARQ-01, OSKEN, NOLITH, VELUNE Paris, BRASCA; Pinterest: Tidy (3D ribbon), Atomic Heart Polymer.

## What "premium" means to her (common denominator)
1. **A giant word + an object that interacts with it.** GAZU (the man walks through the letters), EVORA (the mountain grows out of the letters, fading into fog), Velore (a ring threaded through the "o"), Polymer (a 3D object between the letters). The brand name as architecture, the object on the boundary between foreground and background.
   - CAUTION: the object must be shot on the same background/in the same light (one photo). Cut-outs on a separate background = Hale v5, rejected as "AI". Do the overlap with a photo + the text partly hidden behind a mask of the same photo, or with an object on a plain studio background matching the page colour.
2. **One colour world for the whole page.** Maison Rouge: everything wine red, even the photos. Bilexer: everything misty teal. Epoch Savour: a dark forest + bone. GAZU: grey-black. The palette is taken from the photo and floods the backgrounds; at most 1 colour + its shades + one light.
3. **Serif display + small spaced caps.** Maison Rouge, Evora, Epoch, Nina Elster: a high-contrast serif in headings, tiny labels in caps with +0.1–0.2em tracking, sometimes a thin line before the label. 1–2 italic words as an accent (Lune Noire, Nina Elster, Bilexer). Grotesque/neo-grotesque only for UI.
4. **Atmosphere in the photo.** Fog, smoke, soft focus, film grain, cinematic light (EVORA, ARQ, Bilexer, Maison Rouge, Adwise). Not a "clean stock".
5. **Frame within a frame.** The hero lives in a rounded card on top of a blurred version of the same image (EVORA, ARQ, NOLITH, OSKEN), a window or porthole (VELUNE), a glass block (Maison Rouge). Small floating cards "Explore our work" over the photo.
6. **Soft curves between sections.** Bilexer and Epoch: an arc/wave instead of a straight border, sections "flow".
7. **Lots of air and editorial pacing.** Nina Elster: "gallery calm", generous margins, a centred caption under the work. Velore: "luxury doesn't need to shout".

## What "motion" means to her (HorizonX + video pins)
- Cinematic: a video or slow camera in the hero, "descent"/flight on scroll (VELUNE: "Scroll to descend" through the porthole into Paris).
- A product reveal: an object rotates, light catches it (OSKEN lamp, Polymer, Tidy ribbon).
- The type assembles/parts around the object.
- A scroll progress bar 00—04, numbered chapters.
- Motion is "expensive and slow", not fussy. One big gesture per screen.

## How to apply
- On the first screen, first ask: what is the brand's "word" and what is its "object"? Can they interact (the object in front of/through the word)?
- Choose ONE colour world from the photo and flood the page with it (not white + accent).
- The hero card/frame on top of a blurred background of the same photo is a safe premium move.
- Petal House as an example: the giant "Petal House" with a peony coming out of a letter, the whole page in the bouquet's tone (it already changes with the mood) and a deeper version of the tone in the dark blocks.
