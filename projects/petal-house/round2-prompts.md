# Rebrand: three directions after the references (Maison Rouge / BOUQUET / LÚMEA)
Petal House + the warm-table aesthetic is rejected (generic, AI cliché). The UX is kept: moods, photo before shipping, bag, product page.

Rules for ALL photos: no wooden table, no linen and no kraft by the window. One background and one light per direction. 16:9 unless stated otherwise, maximum resolution. Write at the end: "editorial fashion photography, real flowers, natural imperfections, no text".

---
## Direction A · One colour world (after Maison Rouge, but our colour: deep aubergine-plum)
Common part: "Shot in a studio entirely in deep plum-aubergine (#3B1626): velvet curtain, panelled wall and plinth all in the same colour, one soft key light from the side, rich deep shadows, flowers glowing in the light."
- **A1 hero (16:9)** — a large sculptural bouquet of dark burgundy dahlias, plum calla lilies, blackberry branches and a few white ranunculus in a dark ceramic vase on a plum plinth, the bouquet on the right, empty plum wall on the left.
- **A2 catalogue (4 shots, 1:1)** — the same plinth and background, 4 different bouquets: white ranunculus + burgundy anthurium / pale pink orchids / orange tulips + mimosa / lilac hydrangea. Same framing.
- **A3 wide (21:9)** — a hand in a white lace glove holds a white bouquet out of the plum curtain (like the "Rouge Privé" in the reference, but with our colour).
- **A4 macro (16:9)** — macro of a dark dahlia petal in the light, drops, background in plum darkness.

## Direction B · Grey studio and hands (after BOUQUET)
Common part: "Clean cool light-grey studio wall (#E9E8E5), soft overcast daylight, minimalist, a woman's hand holding the bouquet from the side of the frame, the bouquet wrapped in matte white or tracing paper, Scandinavian editorial look."
- **B1 hero, two halves (two 3:4 shots)** — 1) a woman in a sage-green coat holds a large bouquet of white spray roses on a stone Paris street, overcast; 2) close-up: hands tying a ribbon on a bouquet of tall stems against a grey wall.
- **B2 catalogue (4 shots, 3:4)** — a hand holds a bouquet against a grey wall: pastel tulips and eucalyptus / bright dahlias and grasses / pink peonies in white paper / white calla lilies with a white ribbon.
- **B3 signature (1:1)** — one strange bouquet: a burgundy-green anthurium leaf, white callas, a red ribbon, a hand from the bottom of the frame.
- **B4 process (16:9)** — gloved hands assemble a bouquet on a grey table, stems lie in a neat row.

## Direction C · Night (after LÚMEA)
Common part: "Pure black background, a single narrow beam of light, chiaroscuro, deep shadows, flowers emerging from darkness, Dutch still life mood but modern."
- **C1 hero (21:9)** — macro of a white lily coming out of the darkness on the right, the left half of the frame completely black (space for the name).
- **C2 triptych (three 3:4 shots)** — on a white plinth against a light-grey wall (the contrast with the black site): burgundy anthurium + amaranthus in a white bowl vase / orchids and pink ranunculus with a chocolate ribbon / white chrysanthemums and branches in a smoky glass vase.
- **C3 macro (16:9)** — dark tulip petals in the beam of light, drops.
- **C4 hands (16:9)** — in the dark, only the hands and the bouquet in the light: a florist turns a bouquet, checking it before it ships.
