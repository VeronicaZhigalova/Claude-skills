Petal House, mark and wordmark

Three close concepts. Every mark sits next to Gloock, the site’s heading face, so the logo and the page speak in one voice. Tests: 16 px, one colour, reversed, on kraft.

A · The door

The “house” is a florist’s arched doorway, the “petal” is one bud on a stem growing inside it. The arch echoes the window in our photos. Reads as a shopfront sign, a stamp on kraft, a favicon.
