# Petal House: photo prompts (round 2)

How to generate (Gemini / ChatGPT / Higgsfield, whichever gave blush/bright/white/hands):
- Attach `img/blush.webp` as the reference to every request and write: "Same room, same table, same window light, same camera angle. Change only the flowers."
- Format 16:9, maximum resolution. One frame per request; if the table, window or vase changes, regenerate.
- Name the files by number (`04-lilac.png` …) and send them all together.

## Common part (paste at the start of every prompt)
> Editorial still life photo, bright Parisian florist studio, white plaster wall, tall French window on the left with a wrought-iron balcony and Haussmann building outside, soft warm morning sunlight from the left casting gentle shadows on the wall. An old weathered oak table, kraft paper roll, linen ribbon, black florist scissors and a few fallen petals on the table. Shot on 50mm, f/2.8, natural colours, real petals with small imperfections, no text, no people unless stated.

## A. Bouquets for the catalogue (in the same clear glass vase, in the centre of the table)
1. **04-lilac** — lilac and lavender sweet peas, mauve lisianthus, pale purple clematis, silver-green foliage. Mood: dreamy.
2. **05-deep** — deep burgundy dahlias, dark red garden roses, plum scabiosa, copper beech leaves. Mood: romantic, evening.
3. **06-sunny** — buttery yellow ranunculus, white daisies, yellow tulips, mimosa. Mood: sunny, cheerful.
4. **07-meadow** — loose wild meadow bouquet: cornflowers, chamomile, grasses, poppies. Mood: like it was picked on a walk.
5. **08-tulips** — only tulips, a generous armful of soft pink and peach parrot tulips, stems curving. Mood: seasonal, simple.
6. **09-autumn** — autumn: rust and apricot roses, dried hydrangea, amaranthus, eucalyptus berries.

## B. Story and trust (same studio, same light)
7. **10-phone** — the florist's hands (linen apron, as in hands.webp) hold a phone and photograph the finished blush bouquet on the table. The phone screen is turned away from the camera. → for the "Photo before it ships" block.
8. **11-card** — close-up on the table: a small cream card with a handwritten note, tucked into the linen ribbon of a kraft-wrapped bouquet. The text is illegible, only a hint of handwriting.
9. **12-courier** — outdoors: a Paris street in Saint-Germain on a morning, a courier's hand passing a kraft-wrapped bouquet at a dark green apartment door. Same warm morning sun. The face is not visible.
10. **13-home** — the subscription bouquet at home: a white peony bouquet in the same vase on a linen-covered dining table by a Parisian window, a coffee cup, a book. Same light.

## C. Optional, a nice touch
11. **14-storefront** — the shopfront of a small flower shop on a narrow Saint-Germain street, black-painted façade, buckets of flowers on the pavement, the shop name is not legible.
12. **Video 6–8 s** (Higgsfield / Seedance, start and end = hands.webp): the hands slowly pull the ribbon into a bow, petals move slightly, the camera does not move. Without sound. → it will replace the photo in the "You see it before they do" block.
