    I've never done Pilates. Is that all right?

    Yes. Everyone starts with Foundations. There are six people in the room, so your teacher is next to you most of the class.

    What should I wear?

    Fitted clothes you can move in. We give you grip socks, a towel and water. There are showers and lockers.

    I have back pain or an old injury

    Tell us when you book. For a recent injury or pregnancy we suggest one private first, so we can adapt the group classes for you.

    Can I cancel?

    Free until 12 hours before. After that, the class is taken from your pack.

    Do you have gift cards?

    Yes, for any amount or a ten-class pack. We email it or you pick it up at the studio.
