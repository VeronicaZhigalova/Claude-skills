# Hale Pilates: redesign v3 (22.10)

**The business goal:** more first visits (the €25 trial) that turn into a 10-class pack. Everything on the page leads to "book your first class" and makes it easy to stay.

**Visual language (new, no repeats in project-log):**
- Hedvig Letters Serif + Albert Sans.
- Chalk #F6F5F1, ink #141414, Paris blue #2C43C8 (the only accent), with a pale tint of the blue.
- The previous oat, clay, sage and Libre Caslon look is retired.

## References used
| Section | Source | What we took | Business goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nav | navbar.gallery | The main button carries the price ("First class €25") | The cost is clear before the first click |
| First screen | figmaformotion.com (formula), hero.gallery | One hero object (a client on the reformer) + an offer heading + floating fact cards: the next class with free places, the rating, "6 reformers" | Offer, proof and action in 5 seconds |
| Proof | unsection.com | A rating and three short quotes with the client's name and class, names aligned | Trust before paying |
| Choosing a class | saasframe.io / mobbin.com (onboarding by goal) | A goal → class finder | People who don't know where to start get a starting point |
| Signature move | own idea + seat maps from cinemas and airlines (mobbin) | **Pick your reformer**: a floor plan of the studio with 6 places (by the window / by the mirror), live free places | Proves "only 6 people", turns booking into a small pleasure |
| Prices | pricingpages.design | The recommended pack shows the price per class and that the €25 trial counts towards it | Moves people from the trial to a pack |
| First visit | onepagelove.com | 3 steps with no pressure | Removes the beginner's fear |
| CTA and footer | cta.gallery, footer.design | A final CTA with the nearest free class; a footer with the address, metro and hours | One last chance to act |
| Micro-motion | motionsites.ai | Button fill, a spring when a reformer is chosen | Feedback without noise |

**Motion budget:** the signature move is choosing a reformer (a spring on the chosen place). The floating hero cards appear once. Buttons and states get calm micro-motion. Everything else stays still.
