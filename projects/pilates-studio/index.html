    Before you come

    Good questions.

    I've never done Pilates. Is that all right?

    Yes. Everyone starts with Foundations, whatever their fitness. Classes are six people, so your teacher sees you the whole time.

    What should I wear and bring?

    Comfortable fitted clothes. We give you grip socks, a towel and water. There are showers and lockers.

    I have back pain or an old injury.

    Tell us when you book. For recent injuries or pregnancy we suggest one private session first, so we can adapt the group classes for you.

    Can I cancel?

    Free up to 12 hours before the class. Later than that, the class is used from your pack.

    How often should I come?

    Twice a week is where most people feel the change, usually within a month.

    Do you have gift cards?

    Yes, for any amount or for a ten-class pack, sent by email or printed at the studio.
