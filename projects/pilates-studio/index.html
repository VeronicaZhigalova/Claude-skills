Classes

Small groups, full attention.

Four formats, all on the reformer. Start with Foundations; your teacher will tell you when you are ready to move on.

  • Foundations

    50 min · Beginner

    Springs, breath and alignment. Where everyone starts, whatever their fitness.

  • Flow

    50 min · All levels

    Continuous, unhurried sequences that build length and control.

  • Strength

    50 min · Intermediate

    Heavier springs and slower tempo for deep core and back strength.

  • Restore

    50 min · All levels

    Mobility and breathwork for tired backs and busy weeks.

Private sessions
