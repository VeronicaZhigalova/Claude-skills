Before you book

What time is check-in?

From 4 pm. Check-out is at 11 am. If the days around your stay are free, we’re happy to move this.

How does the deposit work?

We hold €1,000 when you book and return it within 7 days after you leave, once we have checked the house.

Can we bring a dog?

Yes, one dog. Please tell us when you book so we can put a bed and bowls out.

Is there a lifeguard at the pool?

No. The pool has an alarm and a cover, and children should always swim with an adult.
