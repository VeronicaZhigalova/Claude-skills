# Hale Pilates Studio: new site, direction B "Soft Editorial" (22.10)

## Business
- Paris, Le Marais (18 Rue de Turenne). Audience: women 22–35, active.
- Main action: a trial class for €25 → then the 10-class pack (€290, €265 after the trial).
- Classes: Foundations, Flow, Strength, Restore. Prices: €25 first, €32 single, €290 pack, €95 private.
- Booking: our own form with seat selection (6 reformers).
- Tone: soft-premium. Beauty, posture, calm.

## Direction B
- Palette: white #FFFFFF, powder #F2D4CF, chocolate #3A2420, accent burgundy #7E2F3B (italics only and small details).
- Type: Cormorant Garamond (display, italic inserts "made for", "your first") + Hanken Grotesk (text). Not used in previous projects.
- First screen: centred tall portrait (a young woman, calm posture) opens from a narrow vertical strip; on the sides two small cards: "First class €25 / next free class" and "4.9 · 182 reviews"; on scroll the portrait grows to full width.
- Motion (from Figma for Motion / HorizonX / the kit): headline lines rise from a mask; the photo opens up; a slow horizontal ribbon of classes; a giant Hale in the footer. Everything smooth, nothing 3D, no collages.

## Never (lessons from v2–v5)
No composites or cut-outs, no "costume" of the machine, no separate background scene, no strict template.
