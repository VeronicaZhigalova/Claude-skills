Fenn, three other directions

The same home screen in each: Bruno is at grooming, ready at 15:30, and his rabies booster is due in 9 days. Below each phone is how the clinic dashboard would look in the same language. None of them uses the cream, sage and soft serif of the first version, and none repeats a font or palette from the earlier projects.

1 · Clinic signage

Built like hospital wayfinding: a hard grid, numbered panels, labels in mono. Cobalt means "now", orange means "due". It looks like a serious medical service, the opposite of a soft wellness app.
