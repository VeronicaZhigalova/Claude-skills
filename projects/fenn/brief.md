# Fenn: vet clinic + grooming (brief, 2026-10-21)

Portfolio project 7, product case: a client app plus an owner dashboard. Veronika plays the client.

- **Business:** a vet clinic with grooming. One or two clinics in a European city, mid-plus segment.
- **Clients:** pet owners aged 25–45 who value care and convenience. Interface language: English.
- **Pains:**
  1. Clients forget vaccinations and do not come back.
  2. Booking is by phone only: the receptionist is overloaded and no-shows are common.
  3. The owner cannot see the numbers: which services make money and how busy the vets are.
  4. Anxious owners call with questions. They need a live pet status ("in grooming, ready at 15:30") and a chat.
- **Case focus:** a balance of UX and a design system. Key app scenarios, the main dashboard screen, and components with their states (empty, loading, error).
- **Format:** a clickable prototype in code. The app sits in a phone frame and the dashboard is a web page.
- **Balance:** Premium 70 / Wow 30. Calm, trust and warmth. The wow lives in micro-animations and the live pet status.
- **Name:** Fenn. fennvet.com was free per RDAP on 2026-10-21. No clinics or apps under this name turned up in search. Rejected:
  - Bramble: a wildlife app of that name exists, plus a UK practice.
  - Orlavet: too close to the drug Tylavet.
  - Tolvet: close to Tolracol.
