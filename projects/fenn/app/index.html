Fenn design system

Tokens for the app and the clinic dashboard. v4, 21.10.

Colour

Tomato and teal belong to a pet, never to a status. Status uses amber (soon), red (late) and teal-on-light (done).

Type

Big Shoulders Display, uppercase, for names, numbers and headings. Work Sans for everything people read. Space Mono only for labels and times.

Spacing and radius

A 4-point scale. In the app cards have 20 px corners and buttons are pills. In the clinic dashboard everything is square with 2 px rules.

Motion

Two characters. The pet overshoots a little and settles, like a tail: the name, the stamp, the selected time. The clinic moves straight and stops: the dashboard, the tables, the bars. Nothing on the clinic side bounces.

Pet · 600–750 ms · cubic-bezier(.34,1.56,.64,1)

Name slides up 40 px, stamp lands from 1.6×, last paw dot wags every few seconds.

Clinic · 150–350 ms · cubic-bezier(.2,0,0,1)

Bars grow once, toasts appear without sliding, no fades between dashboard states.

Voice

The clinic has three people in it, and they don't sound the same.

Vets · short, exact

"Teeth fine, a bit of tartar at the back. Check again in spring."

Numbers and dates, no exclamation marks.

Groomers and desk · warm, a bit funny

"Tried to eat my towel, so all normal."

One emoji at most, never in anything about health.
