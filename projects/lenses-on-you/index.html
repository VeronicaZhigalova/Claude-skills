FAQ

Do I need a prescription for colour-only lenses?

Not by law in most countries, but see an eye-care professional before your first pair to confirm BC 8.6 and DIA 14.2 fit you.

Will the colour look the same as on the models?

The try-on is close to real life. Light shades look softer on dark eyes, and a natural ring stays around the pupil.

How long does a pair last?

Up to 3 months with daily care. Wear them 8–10 hours a day at most and take them out before sleep.

How does 3 for 2 work?

Put any three pairs in your bag, any shades. The cheapest one is taken off at checkout. Six pairs, two free. The launch offer ends on 8 November.

How fast is delivery?

Orders ship within one working day. Delivery in the EU takes 2–4 working days and is free from €50.

How do I care for my lenses?

Wash your hands, rinse and store the lenses in fresh multipurpose solution every night, and replace the case monthly. Never use tap water.

Can I return a pair?

Unopened blister packs can be returned. Opened lenses can’t be resold for hygiene reasons.
