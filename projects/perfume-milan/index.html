03 The craft

How an Ombra perfume is made

I

Composed

Giulio works on one material at a time. A formula can take him a year, and none has more than forty ingredients.

II

Rested

Eight weeks in the cellar under the shop. The alcohol settles and the sharp edges round off.

III

Poured

We pour by hand through a glass funnel into decanters cut in Murano. A batch is a hundred and twenty bottles.

IV

Numbered

Each bottle gets its name and number engraved on the glass. Batch 07 is on bottle 84.
