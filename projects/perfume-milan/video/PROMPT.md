**Модель:** Kling 3.0 (или Veo 3) → режим Image-to-Video
**Первый кадр:** `start-frame.jpg`  ·  **Длительность:** 10 с  ·  **Формат:** 9:16 (для сайта и мобайла) + повтор в 16:9
**Последний кадр не задавать** (`end-frame-reference.jpg` — только ориентир по настроению: флакон там другого масштаба, модель «поплывёт»).

## Prompt
Locked-off camera, luxury perfume commercial, slow and elegant. The faceted black obsidian stopper of the decanter slowly unscrews by itself, rises a little and is gently set down on its side on the marble beside the bottle. A thin ribbon of pale smoke rises from the open neck into the warm beam of light and curls. Inside the smoke, softly and one by one, ingredients materialise and float down in slow motion to rest on the black marble around the bottle: two dried golden tobacco leaves, a few chunks of glowing amber resin, a folded piece of dark aged leather, a shard of charred oak bark with a faint ember. The bottle itself never moves, its shape and the engraving OMBRA Nº 3 stay exactly the same. Dust floats in the beam. Chiaroscuro, deep black background, cinematic, photorealistic.

## Negative prompt
camera shake, zoom, cut, morphing bottle, changing bottle shape, extra bottles, text changes, hands, people, cartoon, low quality
