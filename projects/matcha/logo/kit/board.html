Rules

Clear space = the height of the «o» counter on every side. Minimum size: wordmark 64 px, symbol 20 px; below 32 px use the two-layer small cut.
Letters are always Mido Green, Black or Rice. Only the layers change colour — always three (two at small sizes), bottom to top as poured.
Don't: stretch, outline, add shadows or gradients, recolour the letters, rotate, or put the colour logo on busy photos (use reversed).
