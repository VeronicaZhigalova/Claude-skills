FAQ

How long does delivery take?

Most orders arrive in 25–40 minutes. We pour as soon as the courier is assigned, so the drink waits for nobody.

Will the layers mix on the way?

A little, and that is part of it. Lids are sealed and ice travels on the side, so it still arrives clearly layered. Give it a stir when you’re ready.

Is the fruit really fresh?

Yes. We crush strawberries, blueberries, mango and banana every morning. No syrups and no flavour powders.

When is delivery free?

From €20, which is two drinks. Below that, delivery is priced by Wolt or Uber Eats at checkout.
